# Retail Sales Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

* Project Overview
This project analyzes retail sales data to identify sales trends, customer patterns, and product category performance using Python.

##  Objectives
- Analyze retail sales data.
- Identify monthly and quarterly sales trends.
- Understand customer demographics.
- Compare revenue across product categories.
- Explore relationships between sales variables.

##  Dataset
- **Source:** [Retail Sales Dataset – Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/mohammadtalib786/retail-sales-dataset)
- **Records:** 1,000 transactions
- **Features:** Transaction ID, Date, Customer ID, Gender, Age, Product Category, Quantity, Price per Unit, and Total Amount.

##  Tools and Technologies
- Python
- Pandas and NumPy
- Matplotlib and Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook / Kaggle Notebook

##  Key Findings
- May 2023 recorded the highest monthly sales, totaling ₹53,150.
- Electronics generated the highest category revenue at ₹156,905.
- Female customers accounted for 51% of transactions.
- Customers aged 46–55 formed the largest age group.
- Price per Unit showed a strong positive relationship with Total Amount.

##  Recommendations
- Review successful sales periods to identify opportunities for future campaigns.
- Monitor revenue across all product categories.
- Develop marketing strategies for different customer age groups.
- Study transaction quantities and pricing to understand purchasing patterns.

##  Limitations
- The dataset contains product categories, not individual product names.
- Revenue does not represent profit because product costs are not provided.
- January 2024 contains limited data, so comparisons for that month may be misleading.

##  Project Files
- `OIBSIP_Retail_Sales_EDA.ipynb` – Notebook containing data analysis, visualizations, and findings.
- `README.md` – Project description and summary.
- `screenshots/` – Screenshots of important outputs and visualizations (add this folder after uploading your screenshots).

##  Conclusion
This analysis provides insights into retail sales trends, customer demographics, and category-level revenue. These findings can support better sales planning and marketing decisions.
