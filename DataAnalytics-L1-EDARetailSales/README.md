# Retail Sales Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

## Project Overview
This project explores retail sales data to identify sales trends, customer patterns, and product-category performance. The goal is to generate insights that can support better business decisions.

## Objectives
- Analyze monthly and quarterly sales.
- Understand customer age-group and gender patterns.
- Compare revenue across product categories.
- Study relationships between numerical variables.
- Provide actionable business recommendations.

## Dataset
**Source:** [Retail Sales Dataset — Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/mohammadtalib786/retail-sales-dataset)

The dataset contains 1,000 transactions with customer details, product categories, quantities, prices, and total transaction amounts.

## Tools Used
- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Kaggle Notebook

## Key Findings
- May 2023 recorded the highest monthly sales in the analyzed data.
- Electronics generated the highest total revenue among the three product categories.
- Customers aged 46–55 formed the largest age group by transaction count.
- Beauty had the highest average transaction value by category.
- Unit price and total transaction amount showed a strong positive correlation.

## Business Recommendations
1. Use sales trends to support inventory and promotional planning.
2. Monitor Electronics and Clothing revenue and maintain suitable stock.
3. Design marketing campaigns for different customer age groups.
4. Test bundles and cross-selling strategies to improve average transaction value.
5. Review sales performance regularly to identify changes in demand.

## Limitations
The dataset contains product categories rather than individual product names, so the ten best-selling individual products cannot be identified from it. January 2024 data may represent only January 1, so it should not be compared directly with complete months.
